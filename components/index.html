Research mode · Conditional projection

Assumes the recent process continues without treatment. The range shows sensitivity to assumed scan noise; it is not a validated probability or a progression diagnosis.

Evidence and assumptions

The trend distribution uses 179 eligible eye pairs from 101 patients. Two visits do not separate true change from measurement error, so four explicit noise assumptions are tested. The same prespecified thresholds are retained. No threshold is lowered to match NKPI.

There are no third scans to validate a forecast based on the first two. Earlier first-scan probability candidates had weak patient-grouped performance (AUROC 0.57 overall; 0.43 in the 4–8-month-only subset). This conditional projection is a different research calculation, not a replacement validated probability model.

Noise settings are scaled to 0.5, 1 and 2 times each domain-component threshold as assumed 95% pair-error limits; a fourth setting tests shared measurement error. These are sensitivity choices, not published device repeatability values. The range is not a confidence interval.
